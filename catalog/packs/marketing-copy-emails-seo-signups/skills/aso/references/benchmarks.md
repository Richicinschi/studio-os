# Sizing impact without outside benchmarks

This file holds no industry figures: conversion rates, rating effects and lift percentages vary by category, country and year, and none can be stated here with a named public source. Never promise a client a specific lift. Measure the app's own baseline instead.

## Conversion from page view to install

Baseline: in the store console, take product page views and installs (or first-time downloads) over the last 28 days, and divide installs by views. Compare with the same app's earlier periods, and by traffic source. Search-result installs that skip the page are counted apart from page-driven ones.

## Ratings

Count the average and number of ratings today, then track both monthly. For a rival comparison, read the same two numbers from the rival's public page. Judge impact by watching your own conversion after a rating change, not by quoting a rule.

## Preview video

Apple autoplays the video muted, so the first seconds carry the message. Google Play autoplays only the first 30 seconds of a video. Test with and without a video where the store allows it, and compare the conversion of each arm.

## Screenshots

The first screenshots do most of the work, since only the first few show in search results. Test the first three before the rest, and compare conversion per variant. Fewer strong screens beat more weak ones; check this with your own test.

## Custom product pages and custom store listings

Both stores let you vary the page by audience (see platform-limits for the documented counts). Measure each version's conversion against the default for the same audience and period.

## Events and promotions

Apple in-app events and Google promotional content bring attention for a limited window. Track impressions, page views and installs before, during and after each one, and compare with a quiet period.

## Reading A/B test results

Read each result against the store tool's own confidence readout first. The bands below are the skill's own rule of thumb, a starting point, not measured values.

| Gain | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Over 10% | Strong winner, apply now |
| 5 to 10% | Meaningful |
| 2 to 5% | Marginal |
| Under 2% | Noise |
