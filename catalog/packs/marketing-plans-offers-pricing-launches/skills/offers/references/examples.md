# Worked before and after sketches

All six are invented illustrations to show method. None reports results, because no real result is attached. Do not quote them as proof, and do not promise buyers a lift. Scores are 1 to 10 guesses made for the exercise.

## 1. Solo UX audit service

Before: "UX audits, 1,200 per site." Scores: outcome 4, belief 3, wait 5, work 6. Weakest: belief.

After:
- Core: "The Five-Day Checkout Review: a prioritised list of the ten fixes that most affect checkout completion, with annotated screenshots."
- Bonuses: a one-hour walkthrough call; a before-launch checklist; a recording of the call.
- Guarantee: if the list has fewer than ten concrete fixes, the fee is returned.
- Scarcity: four reviews a month, next slot dated.
- Name: "The Five-Day Checkout Review."
- Price: half upfront, half on delivery.

Lesson: the price stayed. The name, the fixed scope and the narrow guarantee carried the change.

## 2. Evening photography course

Before: "Learn photography, 10 weeks." Scores: outcome 5, belief 4, wait 4, work 5.

After:
- Core: "Shoot a 20-image portfolio you would show a client in 10 weeks."
- Bonuses: a lighting cheat sheet (speed); graded critiques of two of your images (stuck); a gear-choice guide (decision).
- Guarantee: submit all weekly assignments and the final set; if you do not think it is portfolio-ready, a full refund.
- Scarcity: 14 seats because critiques are done by hand; enrollment closes the Friday before the first class.
- Name: "The Twenty-Frame Portfolio."
- Price: in full, or three monthly parts.

Lesson: name the outcome, tie the limit to a true constraint (marking time), and gate the refund on effort.

## 3. Budget spreadsheet pack

Before: "Spreadsheets for personal budgeting." Scores: outcome 5, belief 5, wait 8, work 5.

After:
- Core: "A working household budget in one evening."
- Bonuses: a ten-minute setup video; a chart showing how the sheets link; free updates.
- Guarantee: thirty days, no questions (fits a low price).
- Scarcity: none needed at this price; an honest launch-week price rise is optional.
- Name: "The One-Evening Budget."
- Price: a single low payment.

Lesson: at small prices the outcome and the fast first win do most of the work, and not every part needs heavy treatment.

## 4. Compliance software for mid-size firms

Before: "Enterprise plan, annual contract, all features, priority support." Scores: outcome 4, belief 5, wait 3, work 4. Weakest: wait.

After:
- Core: "Live in your environment by day 45, with your first audit report generated inside the first quarter."
- Bonuses: an assigned implementation engineer for the first 45 days; ready connectors for the systems in your stack; a training session for your team; quarterly reviews.
- Guarantee: if not live by day 45, billing starts only when it is.
- Scarcity: onboarding slots per quarter, with the next date.
- Name: keep the plan name, add the engagement "Guided Rollout."
- Price: same annual figure, billed quarterly, with a short paid pilot first.

Lesson: for large buyers the time to value is the offer, so state it and back it with terms.

## 5. Peer group for freelancers

Before: "Monthly calls for freelancers." Scores: outcome 4, belief 4, wait 6, work 6.

After:
- Core: "Ten freelancers, one goal each, one hour a fortnight, a group that has seen your problem before."
- Bonuses: an intake call to set your goal; a library of frameworks contributed by past members; a mid-term check-in; a year of alumni access.
- Guarantee: leave after the first two sessions with a refund of the unused balance.
- Scarcity: group capped at ten.
- Name: "The Freelance Table."
- Price: per quarter, or in three parts.

Lesson: the room is the product, and renewal-friendly extras (alumni access) change the question from "one quarter?" to "a year?".

## 6. Small content agency

Before: "Blog writing retainer, four articles a month." Scores: outcome 3, belief 4, wait 3, work 6.

After:
- Core: "We run your content: a published plan, articles tied to search topics, a monthly report of what drew visitors and enquiries."
- Bonuses: a one-time audience research session; a quarterly refresh list of older posts; vetted writers with an edit pass; a quarterly summary meeting.
- Guarantee: the first month is a paid pilot (four published pieces and a topic roadmap); if the plan does not convince you, we part ways with nothing owed beyond the pilot.
- Scarcity: three retainers started per quarter.
- Name: tier "Growth Retainer", entry engagement "The First-Month Content Plan."
- Price: monthly, with a lower rate for a longer commitment.

Lesson: slow-moving services must address time to result in the offer itself. A pilot with a checkpoint sells better than "trust us, wait six months."

## What the six share

| Case | Main move | Second move |
|---|---|---|
| UX audit | Named and scoped | Narrow guarantee |
| Photography course | Outcome framing | Effort-gated refund |
| Spreadsheet pack | Fast first win | Plain guarantee |
| Compliance software | Time-to-value commitment | Billing tied to go-live |
| Peer group | Sold the room | Alumni access |
| Content agency | Outcome plus timeline | Paid pilot |

In each, the structure moved and the price barely did. Price is the comparison; the offer is the value.
