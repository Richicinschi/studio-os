# Benchmarks: set your own

No outside averages are quoted here, because results depend on audience, niche, traffic and offer. Log your own from day one and treat the first month as your baseline.

## Landing page conversion
Count sign-ups divided by unique visitors to the page, per format and per traffic source, over the same period. Compare:
- Formats against each other (checklist, template, ebook, quiz, webinar registration, mini-course, free trial). Expect more commitment to mean fewer sign-ups but better leads.
- Sources against each other: content upgrade inside a post, dedicated page with organic traffic, dedicated page with paid traffic, social link, exit-intent popup, sidebar or banner.
- Your own pages by audience or industry, if you serve more than one.

For a webinar, also count the share of registrants who attend.

## Good and weak lead signs
Good: opens of the first three emails above your list's usual open rate, clicks to content or product pages, a return visit soon after, a fit with the target profile, a trial, demo or purchase later. Pick the time windows from your own sales cycle and write them down before you measure.
Weak: unsubscribing within three emails, never opening past the delivery email, throwaway addresses, no fit with the target profile, interest in the file only.

Volume against quality: a generic ebook brings many mediocre leads; a specific template brings fewer, better ones; a quiz brings many and can be segmented; a webinar brings few strong ones; a checklist brings many, middling ones; a free trial brings the fewest and the strongest. Check this against your own lead-to-customer rate per magnet.

## Cost per lead
Divide what you spent on a channel (ad spend, tool fees, your hours at your own hourly value) by the leads it brought, over the same period. Compare organic search, blog content upgrades, partner co-promotion and each paid channel. Organic channels are slow to build, so judge them over a longer window.

## Cost and time to create
Keep a note of your real hours and any freelancer or tool invoice for each magnet you build. For a new format, ask two freelancers for a quote and use your own past builds as the estimate. A quick format (checklist, cheat sheet) is small work, a template or swipe file a working day or so, and ebooks, quizzes, mini-courses and webinars take longer; a team builds faster than one person. Treat these as relative sizes, not measured durations.

## Time to results
First leads can come right away if you already have traffic or run paid promotion. Meaningful volume, organic growth and any pipeline effect come later. Decide the review dates in advance (first read, volume check, full return check) and compare each against your own baseline.
