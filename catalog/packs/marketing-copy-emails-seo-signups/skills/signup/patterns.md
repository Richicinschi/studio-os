# Signup flow patterns by business type

Pick the nearest pattern, then trim it to what your product truly needs.

## Business software with a trial
1. Work email plus password, or one-click sign in with a work identity provider.
2. Name and team or company. Role only if it changes what you show first.
3. Straight into guided first use.

## Consumer app
1. One tap with a platform account, or email alone.
2. Land in the product itself.
3. Ask for profile details later, when a feature needs them.

## Waitlist or early access
1. Email only.
2. One optional question about the visitor's goal, to help you prioritise.
3. A confirmation that says when and how they will hear from you.

## Online shop
1. Guest checkout is the default path.
2. Offer to create an account after the order, using details already entered.
3. Alternatively, one-click sign in with a platform account.

## Rules that apply to every pattern
- A visible way to delete the account must exist once the account does. Link it from settings and say so in the privacy note.
- Never promise "no card needed" or a trial length the billing setup does not honour.
- Whatever you defer from the form must have a place where it is collected later.
