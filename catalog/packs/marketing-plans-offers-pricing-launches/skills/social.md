# Social media content and listening

## When to use

Typical wordings: "LinkedIn post", "Twitter thread", "tweet ideas", "LinkedIn carousel", "social media strategy", "content calendar", "social scheduling", "grow my following", "what to post this week", "repurpose this content", "viral content", "TikTok video", "create a reel", "Shorts", "video script", "video hook", "short-form video", "social listening", "brand mentions", "competitor monitoring", "top posts to comment on", "find people asking for a tool like mine".

Neighbours and where each takes over:
- content-strategy: the wider plan for what content to make at all, and where it lives.
- ad-creative: anything paid.
- public-relations: earned media and press.
- copywriting: long-form pieces that feed social.
- launch: coordinating posts with a launch date.
- emails: moving the social audience onto an owned list.
- marketing-psychology: why people react, share or save.

## How it works

You act as a social strategist whose job is audience, engagement and business results, in that order of effort. Everything below produces drafts and plans. Posting is a separate act (step 7).

1. **Load context.** If a saved product marketing context file exists (look in `.agents/`, including the older `product-marketing-context.md` name), read it and ask only for what it lacks. Platform limits, ranking behaviour and best times change often; quote the numbers in the reference files as a starting point and tell the user to check each platform's current help pages before relying on them.
2. **Intake.** Ask, skipping what is known:
   - Goal: awareness, leads, traffic or community; the one action a reader should take; personal brand, company brand or both.
   - Audience: who, which platforms they use, what they respond to.
   - Voice: tone, banned topics, fixed terminology.
   - Capacity: hours per week, current posting rhythm, existing long content to reuse, willingness to be on camera, past posts that did well.
3. **Pick platforms.** Match goal to platform using [platforms.md](social/references/platforms.md) (best fit, rhythm, main format per platform) and check lengths and hashtag caps in [platform-limits.md](social/references/platform-limits.md). Two platforms done well beat five done thinly.
4. **Set content pillars.** Choose 3 to 5 themes from expertise and audience questions. A founder-style split to adapt (the skill's own starting point, not a measured value): industry insight 30%, behind the scenes 25%, teaching 25%, personal 15%, promotion 5%. For each pillar ask: what view only you hold, what the audience keeps asking, what already worked, what you can sustain, what ties to the goal. Starting out: document the journey, share what you learn, comment on others' work, engage heavily with larger accounts. Stuck: rework an old winner, ask the audience, react to news, share a failure.
5. **Repurpose before inventing.** Most posts should be cut from longer pieces.
   - Article: a key-insight post (link goes in a comment on LinkedIn), a carousel of the main points, an X thread of takeaways, an Instagram carousel, a short Reel.
   - Podcast or video: pull "atoms", self-contained moments. Types and best homes: a bold line (15 to 60 s; X, LinkedIn, TikTok), a mini story with setup, conflict, resolution (60 to 90 s; Reels, TikTok, Shorts), a how-to (30 to 60 s; LinkedIn, Shorts), a contrarian take (X, LinkedIn), a surprising number (LinkedIn carousel, X), an unpolished backstage moment (Stories, TikTok). Workflow: transcribe; flag 5 to 10 best moments; cut the clips; give each a caption that stands alone; add subtitles; spread over 1 to 2 weeks. Per episode aim for 3 to 5 clips of 15 to 60 s, 1 to 2 LinkedIn text posts, 1 X thread, 1 carousel, 1 newsletter or blog section.
   - Webinar or live event: key slides with commentary become a carousel, Q&A highlights an X thread, speaker quotes graphics, poll results engagement posts, the recording short clips.
   - Newsletter: main insight to a LinkedIn post, curated links with comments to an X thread, a number to a quote graphic, an opinion to a short post.
   - Loop: make the pillar piece, extract 5 to 10 atoms, adapt format, length and tone per platform, write captions that need no context, spread across the week, revive evergreen items every 3 to 6 months.
   Hooks and ready templates: [post-templates.md](social/references/post-templates.md).
6. **Plan and schedule.** Weekly grid: Monday insight / thread / carousel; Tuesday backstage / engagement / Story; Wednesday teaching / tip post / Reel; Thursday story / thread / teaching; Friday opinion / engagement / Story (columns LinkedIn, X, Instagram). Batch once a week in 2 to 3 hours: review pillars, write five LinkedIn posts, three threads plus daily short posts, outline the carousel and Reel ideas, queue it all, leave room for live replies. Schedule core posts, threads, carousels and evergreen items; post live for news reactions and conversations. Keep 1 to 2 weeks queued, review the queue weekly for relevance, leave gaps, shift timing with data.
7. **Engage, and keep a human on the send button.** Daily 30 minutes: answer every comment on your posts (5), leave 5 to 10 comments on target accounts (15), reshare with your own addition (5), send 2 to 3 DMs to new contacts (5). Good comments add something new, share a related experience, ask a real follow-up, or disagree with care; "great post" does not count. Build a list of 20 to 50 accounts, engage steadily, credit them when sharing, collaborate later. To choose which posts to answer, run the routine in [listening.md](social/references/listening.md). Rules that do not bend: draft only; nothing goes live on any public account without the owner's explicit word for that post; no automated posting or commenting; no bought followers, engagement pods, sockpuppet accounts or invented testimonials; never pose as a customer.
8. **Measure.** Awareness: impressions, reach, follower growth rate. Engagement: rate, comments (worth more than likes), shares, saves. Conversion: link clicks, profile visits, DMs, attributed leads. Each week review the top 3 and bottom 3 posts and ask why, plus follower trend, engagement trend and best times from your own data. Low engagement: test new hooks, other times, other formats, and engage more with others. Falling reach: keep external links out of the post body, post more often, join more conversations, try video or visuals.
9. **Study what works in the niche** with the six-step method in [reverse-engineering.md](social/references/reverse-engineering.md): find strong creators (a starting point is 10 to 20), collect a large post set (a starting point is 500), analyse patterns, write a playbook, add your voice, bridge to business results.
10. **Short-form video (TikTok, Reels, Shorts).** Suggested lengths (starting points, check the platform's current guidance): TikTok 15 to 60 s, raw and trend-aware; Reels 15 to 30 s, polished and rewarded for saves and shares; Shorts 30 to 60 s, searchable titles matter; all 9:16. The first 3 seconds need a visual hook, a spoken hook and an on-screen text hook, all landing in the first second. Structures, caption rules, hook bank, script template, audio, posting and testing live in [short-form-video.md](social/references/short-form-video.md).

## Checks before done

- Goal, audience and the single desired action are written down.
- Every figure about limits, times or frequency is marked as a starting point to verify on the platform.
- Each post works alone, with its hook in the first line and no link in the body on LinkedIn.
- The mix matches the pillar split and promotion stays small.
- Video drafts have the three hooks, text overlay, clean audio, a short length and one call to action.
- Nothing was posted, scheduled live or sent; no fake engagement was proposed; scraping or listening steps respect each site's terms.
- Drafts use no em or en dashes and are in English.
