# Onboarding experiment menu

Each line is an idea with the question it answers. Run one change per test and watch activation rate first.

## Flow and friction
- Verify email before versus after the first result.
- Empty screen versus pre-filled sample content or templates.
- Social or single-sign-on login to cut typing.
- Fewer required steps; optional versus required fields; a skip link on non-critical steps.
- Step order: highest-value first, hard steps later, one path versus branching by answer, quick start versus full setup.

## Progress and motivation
- Progress bar versus none; "n% done" wording; a bar that starts partly filled instead of empty.
- A short checklist versus a long one.
- Badges, streaks, rewards, features that unlock gradually; leaderboards only where comparison suits the audience.
- Success messages after key actions, suggested next step after each completion, a preview of the outcome.

## Guidance
- Interactive tours made with an interactive demo tool, tooltip versus modal, short versus long tour, automatic start versus user-started, self-paced versus guided.
- Video for complex workflows.
- Button wording, placement, contrast, sticky versus scrolling, and whether a secondary "learn more" link helps or distracts.
- Hotspots, coachmarks, feature announcements, contextual help, search versus guided discovery.

## Personalisation
- Separate paths by role, goal, industry or experience level.
- Role-specific default dashboards and templates.
- Welcome message using name, company or role; examples from the user's industry; benchmarks for their sector.
- Highlight only the features related to their stated goal.

## Help
- Free onboarding call for complex products, live chat during setup, proactive outreach to people who stall.
- Help articles, videos, early access to a peer community.

## Email and feedback
- Founder-signed welcome versus generic; behaviour-triggered versus calendar-triggered; send timing and frequency; short tip format; video in the email.
- Subject lines, depth of personalisation, one clear button, testimonial inclusion, trial-ending reminders, plain text versus designed.
- When to ask for a satisfaction score and what to do with it; a single question such as what is getting in the way; thumbs up or down on features; survey timing; a reward for answering.

## Recovery
- Timing of the first nudge; human versus automated; a shorter path for returners; a discount or longer trial; asking what blocked them; offering a live walkthrough.
- On return: welcome-back text, resume where they left off, a summary of what changed, option to restart, remaining trial time.

## Technical and access
- Faster load and progressive loading; graceful error recovery; offline behaviour on mobile.
- Mobile: tap target size, swipe navigation, fewer screens, easier input, permission timing.
- Screen reader support, keyboard-only use, colour contrast, text size.

## What to record in every test
Activation rate, time to activation, completion of each step, where people leave, return rate, day 1, 7 and 30 retention, which features get used, support requests during onboarding.
