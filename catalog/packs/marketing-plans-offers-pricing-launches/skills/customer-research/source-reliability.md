# How far to trust each source

| Source | Weight | Distortion to name in the report |
|---|---|---|
| Unprompted interview remarks | Very high | Few people; engaged customers over-represented |
| Win/loss interviews | High | Fresh memory only; people tidy their reasons afterwards |
| Review sites and app store reviews | High | Mostly people who love or hate |
| Reddit and other community posts | Medium to high | Technical, doubtful, loud minority |
| Support tickets | Medium | Problems only; the silent majority never writes in |
| Survey free text | Medium | Shaped by how the question was worded |
| Survey closed questions | Low to medium | Mirrors the options you offered |
| NPS comments | Medium | Follow the score, written at a prompted moment |
| Video and short-clip comments | Medium | Engaged viewers, partly performing |
| Audience-tool data | Medium to high | Strong on what people do, silent on why |
| Job postings | Low to medium | Describe a wish, not today's pain |

Rule of thumb for leading with confidence: state the label first, then the evidence count ("High: 9 of 14 interviews plus two review sites"). A label of Low should say what extra evidence would lift it.

Age: last 12 months is the main body, 12 to 24 months needs a caveat, older is background. A theme found in both old and new data is durable.
