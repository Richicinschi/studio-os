# Tools and fraud

## Tool groups

Referral platforms: ReferralCandy (shops), Ambassador (enterprise), Friendbuy (shops and subscriptions), GrowSurf (software), Mention Me, Viral Loops (templates). General tools you may already own (a payments system, a CRM, an event pipeline) can carry tracking data, but check what each one really supports before relying on it for referral attribution.

Affiliate networks and managers: Impact, PartnerStack, Tapfiliate, FirstPromoter, Rewardful, Tolt, Refersion. Partner relationship tools with deal registration and tiers: Introw. Link tracking and partner programs: Dub. ShareASale is not listed on purpose: its owner, Awin, announced that the platform would close once its users had moved to Awin, so evaluate Awin instead.

Vendors merge, close and change their products, so check each vendor's current site before recommending a tool. Paid plans: prices change, so ask the user before signing up for anything and quote the fee.

## Choosing

Compare: fit with your payment system, fraud detection, payout handling, reporting, customization, and price against the size of your program.

## Fraud patterns

- Self-referral with fake accounts
- Rings of people referring each other
- Coupon sites posting referral codes
- Throwaway email addresses
- VPN or device spoofing

## Controls

Technical: require email verification, fingerprint devices, watch IP addresses, pay only after activation, set a minimum activity bar.

Policy: clear terms, cap referrals per period, claw back rewards on refund or chargeback, review odd patterns by hand.

Structure: make the referred person do something meaningful, cap lifetime rewards, pay in product credit rather than cash since it attracts fewer fraudsters.
