```markdown
## Positioning and messaging
Main promise (headline plus subheadline), target audience, positioning angle (for example simplest, enterprise-grade, all-in-one), 3 recurring message themes with the page each came from.

## Product and features
Core capabilities, claimed differentiators, integration count and the top few, product-direction signals from the changelog.

## Pricing
| Tier | Price | Includes |
Then: billing options, trial, quirks (per seat, usage-based, add-ons).

## Customers and proof
Named customers, industries, case-study themes, review ratings per source with counts.

## SEO and content
Traffic, traffic value and keyword strength, top 3 pages with keyword and traffic, posting rate, content types and topics, backlink profile and whether it grows, holds or shrinks.

## Strengths and weaknesses
Three each, each with its evidence source.

## What this means for us
Where they beat us, where we beat them, openings, threats.

## Raw data sources
Raw folder, plus the date of each pull.
```

## Quick scan profile

Header with URL and date, then: a short at-a-glance table (tagline, audience, entry price, free plan or trial, domain rank, traffic, top-10 keywords, referring domains); the exact homepage headline and subheadline plus a one or two sentence positioning note; a pricing table; a 2 to 3 sentence takeaway on what is notable, strong and weak.

## Summary file

```markdown
# Competitive landscape summary
Generated, your product, number profiled.

| Dimension | Us | Rival A | Rival B | Rival C |
|-----------|----|---------|---------|---------|
rows: tagline, audience, positioning, entry price, free tier, domain rank, est. traffic, referring domains, review rating, main strength, main weakness
```

Follow with the landscape paragraph, the positioning map, 3 to 5 takeaways and the gaps.

## Positioning map

Pick the two axes that fit the market, then place every rival and your own product.

| Market | X axis | Y axis |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Software tools | easy to powerful | budget to premium |
| Developer tools | low-code to code-first | solo to team |
| Business platforms | small-firm to enterprise | single-purpose to platform |
| Content tools | templated to custom | self-serve to managed |

Draw a cross with the axis labels at the ends, one name per quadrant position, then write 1 to 2 sentences on what the map shows and where the empty space is.

## SWOT per competitor

Four lists, always from our product's point of view:
- Their strengths against us (honest).
- Their weaknesses against us (with evidence).
- Opportunities for us: gaps in their offer, segments they ignore, message angles they miss.
- Threats from them: fast-improving areas, features overlapping ours, market moves that could change perception.
