# Ideas 1 to 44: content, search, tools, ads, social

## Content and search (1-10)

1. **Low-competition keywords**: aim at long, odd or brand-new search phrases big sites skip, so you can reach page one fast.
2. **Public site audit**: audit your own site's technical search health and write up each fix as you go.
3. **Glossary pages**: one page per industry term, each answering a "what does X mean" query.
4. **Template pages at scale**: one layout filled from a data set for a repeating query pattern, such as city, versus or integration pages.
5. **Content repurposing**: turn one article into a thread, a short video, an audio episode and a graphic.
6. **Own-data reports**: publish findings from your product's data; nobody else can copy them and others will link to them.
7. **Internal links**: connect related pages in topic clusters so authority flows and crawlers find everything.
8. **Refresh old posts**: update numbers and examples in pages that already rank; this often beats writing new ones.
9. **Help-center search**: write support articles around the problems people type into search.
10. **Borrowed authority**: publish on large platforms such as blogging networks and professional sites, which rank faster than a new domain.

## Competitors (11-13)

11. **Versus pages**: "your tool against theirs" pages catch people close to buying.
12. **Judo on rivals**: turn a competitor's weak moment, such as a price rise, into your campaign.
13. **Ad spying**: study rivals' ads in public ad libraries or monitoring tools to see which messages hold up.

## Free tools and building (14-22)

14. **Side projects**: small useful apps near your topic that draw people who later try the main product.
15. **Engineering as marketing**: free calculators, checkers or generators that lead naturally to the paid tool.
16. **Competitor importers**: a "bring your data from X" button that removes the cost of switching.
17. **Quizzes**: interactive tests and assessments that sort leads and get shared.
18. **Calculators**: ROI, cost or savings tools that earn links and rank for practical queries.
19. **Browser extensions**: standalone helpers that also give you a store listing as a channel.
20. **Microsites**: a separate small site per campaign or audience, which can rank faster than a section of the main site.
21. **Free scanners**: tools that check a site, setup or file and report problems.
22. **Open APIs**: let outside developers build on you so an ecosystem forms.

## Paid ads (23-34)

Ad formats, objectives and targeting options change often and differ by country. Check each platform's current help pages before promising a person a format or a budget floor.

23. **Podcast sponsorship**: buy slots on shows your buyers hear; host-read spots work best.
24. **Awareness before conversion**: run reach ads first so the later sales ads meet a warmer crowd.
25. **Facebook ads**: fine audience targeting, creative tests and retargeting on the Meta network.
26. **Instagram ads**: image-led products, using Stories and Reels placements.
27. **X ads**: promoted posts and follower campaigns aimed at people discussing your field.
28. **LinkedIn ads**: target job title, company size and industry; higher prices pay off for B2B.
29. **Reddit ads**: speak plainly to enthusiast communities; honesty is rewarded there.
30. **Quora ads**: show up beside questions your product answers.
31. **Search ads**: bid on brand, competitor and category searches from people ready to act.
32. **YouTube ads**: video placed before or among related content with narrow targeting.
33. **Retargeting across sites**: follow visitors across networks with one consistent message.
34. **Click-to-chat ads**: ads that open a message thread instead of a landing page.

## Social and community (35-44)

35. **Own community**: run a chat group or forum around the product and the problem it solves.
36. **Q&A answers**: give real, expert answers on question sites and mention the product only where it fits.
37. **Mining Reddit language**: read threads to collect the exact words and complaints of your audience.
38. **Reddit participation**: join relevant subreddits honestly and give value before any mention.
39. **LinkedIn personal brands**: founders and staff posting useful views to reach B2B buyers.
40. **Instagram presence**: behind-the-scenes and customer stories for visual products.
41. **X presence**: steady useful posts and threads to grow a following.
42. **Short video**: TikTok, Reels and Shorts clips that reach new people.
43. **Engagement groups**: peers agree to react to each other's posts to lift reach.
44. **Comment marketing**: sharp, useful comments on others' popular posts to gain visibility.
