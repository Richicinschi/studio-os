# Signup experiments worth running

Change one thing per test. Decide the success metric (completed signups, or activated users) before you start.

## Structure
- One screen against several screens, and several screens with a progress bar against without.
- One column against two.
- Form inline on the landing page against a dedicated page.
- Labels above fields against beside them.

## Fields
- Email and password only, against adding name.
- Phone present, absent, or optional.
- One full-name box against first and last.
- Company asked now against asked in first use.
- Balance of required and optional fields.

## Ways to sign in
- Add a platform account button, and test which providers your audience uses.
- Provider buttons above the email form against below it.
- Provider-only against provider plus email.

## Look
- Button size and contrast.
- Plain page against product imagery.
- Card-style form against bare form.
- A layout built for small screens first.

## Copy
- Headline above the form.
- Button wording: "Create account", "Start trial", "Get going", or a wording that states the trial length.
- Brief against descriptive labels, placeholder wording, tone of error messages.
- Password rules shown up front against shown on error.
- Proof next to the form, security or compliance marks, a line on card requirements, a line on privacy.

## Trial and commitment
- Card required against not required.
- Trial length: try a short, a medium and a long period.
- Free plan against time-limited trial.
- Limited trial against full access.

## Friction
- Verify email before use, after first use, or never.
- Bot challenge on or off.
- Terms checkbox against a notice under the button.
- Phone check for high-value accounts only.

## After submit
- A clear "what now" message.
- Instant access against confirmation first.
- Welcome message that uses the answers given.
- Automatic sign-in after signup against asking for the password again.
