# Platforms and health signals

## Choosing a platform

| Platform | Good fit | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Discord | Developers, gamers, creators; live chat | Noisy, poor search, a hurdle for new joiners |
| Slack | Business and professional groups; buyers already use it | Free plan limits history; feels like office work |
| Circle | Course or creator communities; tidy interface | Little organic discovery; you must bring the traffic |
| Reddit | Large public groups; helps search visibility | Not your property; hard to moderate |
| Facebook Groups | Consumer brands, older audiences | Falling organic reach; depends on the algorithm |
| Discourse forum | Long technical discussions; strong for search | Slower pace; posting takes more effort |

Before posting a brand's own links or promotion anywhere, read that platform's or sub-community's self-promotion rules.

## Weekly health signals

- DAU/MAU ratio, a measure of stickiness. Record it weekly and compare it with your own earlier weeks; do not judge it against a generic target.
- Share of new members who post within 7 days of joining.
- Share of threads that get at least one reply.
- Lurker ratio: members who joined but have not posted in 30 days or more.
- Share of posts written by people outside the company team.

## Warning signs

The thresholds below are the skill's own starting points, not measured values. Tune them to your community's baseline.

- Most posts come from staff rather than members.
- Questions sit unanswered for more than 24 hours.
- Five people or fewer account for 80 percent or more of the activity.
- Newcomers write an intro and then go quiet.
