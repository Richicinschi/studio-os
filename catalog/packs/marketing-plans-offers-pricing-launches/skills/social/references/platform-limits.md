# Limits and hashtag caps

The caps and text limits are carried over from the earlier version of this skill and were not re-measured; the suggested-tag column is the skill's own starting point, not a measured value. Check the platform's own help pages before depending on any figure here; limits move. The Instagram five-tag cap was announced by Instagram in December 2025 per press coverage, and the TikTok five-tag cap and 4,000 character caption are reported by third-party guides only; the Pinterest, Threads and YouTube rows were not re-checked at all.

| Platform | Hashtag cap | Suggested tags | Text cap | Shown before "more" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 5 | 3 to 5 | 2,200 characters | about 125 characters |
| Facebook | none official | 1 to 2 | 63,206 characters | the first lines only |
| TikTok | 5 (since August 2025) | 3 to 5 | 4,000 characters | about 150 characters |
| LinkedIn | none official | 3 to 5 | 3,000 characters | about 210 characters |
| Twitter/X | none official | 1 to 2 | 280 standard, 25,000 on the premium tier | whole post |
| YouTube | 15 (above that, all tags are ignored) | 3 to 5 (Shorts: 1 to 5) | title 100 (about 70 shown), description 5,000 | about 100 characters of description |
| Pinterest | 20 per pin | 2 to 5 | title 100, description 500 | about 50 characters on desktop |
| Threads | 1 topic tag per post | 1 | 500, or 10,500 with an expandable text attachment | first 1 to 2 lines |

Notes:
- On YouTube the first three description hashtags appear above the title as links.
- Pinterest has moved away from hashtags; write the description as natural sentences with the search terms in them.
- Threads is not tag-driven; it mixes followed accounts with recommendations.

Habits that follow from the numbers:
- Tags count toward the character limit everywhere.
- Put the point before the cut-off where text gets folded.
- On Instagram and TikTok a few tags now do better than a pile.
- On LinkedIn, tags at the end of a post do better than tags inside it.
- On Facebook tags barely affect reach; use sparingly.
