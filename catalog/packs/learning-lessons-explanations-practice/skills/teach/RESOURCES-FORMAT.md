# RESOURCES.md format

`RESOURCES.md` is the short list of sources this course trusts. Teach from it, not from what you half remember. The communities in it are where the learner goes to build wisdom.

## Structure

```md
# {Topic} Resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: _Daniels' Running Formula_, Jack Daniels](https://example.com)
  Training paces and plans built on measured fitness. Use for: setting pace targets and weekly structure.
- [Article: "Why Easy Runs Matter", Sample Author, Example Journal](https://example.com)
  Review of evidence on low-intensity volume. Use for: justifying easy days to a doubtful learner.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/AdvancedRunning](https://reddit.com/r/AdvancedRunning)
  Well-moderated forum with experienced runners. Use for: plan reviews and plateau advice.
- Local: Thursday track group at {club name}
  Use for: coaching on form, live.
```

## Rules

- **Trust must be high.** Favour primary sources, known experts, reviewed research and well-run communities. Skip anything that is advertising dressed up as teaching.
- **Annotate every entry** with one line on what it covers and when to use it. In a few months a bare link will mean nothing.
- **Split into Knowledge and Wisdom,** as in the teaching skill ([teach.md](../teach.md)). An entry may belong to just one group.
- **Admit gaps.** If the mission needs an area that has no good source yet, list it under a `## Gaps` heading so the next search knows what to look for.
- **Remove weak entries.** A source that proved wrong, thin or off-topic should be deleted, not left to hide among the rest. Five good ones beat thirty poor ones.
- **Note opt-outs.** If the learner has said no to communities, write that here so you stop suggesting them.
