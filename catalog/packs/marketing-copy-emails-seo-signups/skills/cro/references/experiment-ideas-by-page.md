# Experiment ideas by page

A menu of tests, grouped by page. Choose the few that answer a problem found in the audit; each line is "what to change: what you are trying to learn".

## Homepage

Hero
- Headline: concrete wording against abstract wording.
- Subheadline: whether adding or sharpening one helps the headline land.
- Above-the-fold CTA: present or absent.
- Hero visual: screenshot, animated clip, illustration or video.
- Button colour: contrast and visibility.
- Button wording: "Try It Free", "Begin" or "Watch a Tour".
- Interactive demo: let people touch the product straight away.

Proof
- Logo strip in the hero or lower down.
- A case-study result shown at the top.
- Badges for security, compliance or awards.
- A visitor count in the headline.
- Testimonials high on the page or in their own block.
- Video testimonials against written quotes.

Content
- Icon grid with short text against fuller feature sections.
- Reordering so the most valuable features come first.
- Adding or removing secondary CTAs down the page.
- Leading with outcomes instead of capabilities.
- A comparison block against rivals or doing nothing.

Navigation and UX
- Sticky menu with a CTA.
- Menu order: key items at the ends.
- A highlighted button in the menu.
- Live chat against an AI assistant.
- A footer that offers clearer secondary actions.
- An exit-intent offer for leaving visitors.

## Pricing page

Presentation
- Annual price shown first or monthly price shown first.
- Price endings such as 49, 50 or 47.
- A "most popular" tag on the plan you want chosen.
- Two, three or four visible tiers.
- Plan order, to set an anchor.
- A listed enterprise tier or a "contact sales" box.

Usability
- Calculator for usage-based pricing.
- A guided wizard against a comparison table.
- Feature table against collapsible sections.
- Monthly/annual switch with the saving called out.
- A short quiz that recommends a plan.
- Number of steps between picking a plan and paying.

Objections
- FAQ aimed at price worries.
- ROI calculator.
- Money-back promise in a prominent spot.
- Per-seat breakdown for team plans.
- Clear listing of what each tier includes.
- Side-by-side value against competitors.

Proof
- Quotes about return on investment.
- Customer logos beside the prices.
- Review-site ratings.
- A case result that talks about cost and value.

## Demo request page

Form
- Fewer fields.
- One step against several steps with a progress bar.
- Form high on the page or after the pitch.
- Phone field required, optional or absent.
- Auto-filling or hiding fields you can look up.
- Labels above fields or inside them.

Content
- Benefits ahead of the form.
- A preview clip of the demo.
- A "what you will see" list.
- Testimonials next to the form.
- FAQ underneath.
- Video against text for explaining the value.

CTA and routing
- Button text: "Pick a Time" against "Book a 20-Min Chat".
- An instant on-demand demo next to the live one.
- Messaging tailored to the visitor's source.
- Navigation removed.
- Calendar embedded inline or linked out.
- Routing: small accounts self-serve, larger ones to sales.

## Blog and resource pages

Post CTAs
- Sticky floating CTA.
- Inline CTAs against one at the end only.
- Showing estimated reading time.
- Related reading at the foot of a post.
- Free access against a gate.
- A content upgrade specific to the article.

Resource hub
- Better filtering and navigation.
- Site search.
- Featured picks.
- Grid against list.
- Bundles grouped by theme.
- Gating some items and tracking downloads.

## Landing pages

Message match
- Headline copied from the ad.
- Visual that echoes the ad creative.
- The same offer the ad made.
- Separate pages per audience segment.

Focus
- No navigation.
- CTA repeated down the page.
- Embedded form against a button that leads to one.
- Urgency or scarcity wording, only when it is true.
- How much proof, and where.
- A video that explains the offer.

Length
- Short against long.
- Only above-the-fold content.
- Reordered sections, most important first.
- No footer links.

## Feature pages

Showing the feature
- Screenshot or demo of it working.
- Examples of how customers use it.
- Before and after view of the impact.
- Video walk-through.
- An interactive try-out with no signup.

Next step
- A trial offer tied to this feature.
- Links to related features.
- Comparison with competitors' version.
- A pointer to the plan that includes it.
- A success story about this feature.

## Across the whole site

Site-wide
- Chat widget on or off.
- Cookie banner design that adds the least friction.
- Page speed against richer features.
- Mobile layout polish.
- Accessibility improvements.
- Content personalised by segment.

Navigation
- Menu structure and labels.
- Where search sits.
- A conversion CTA that is always visible.
- Breadcrumbs.
