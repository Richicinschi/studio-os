# Ideas 45 to 86: email, partners, events, press, launches

## Email (45-53)

45. **Honest "we slipped" emails**: when you truly err, say so; candour brings replies.
46. **Reactivation campaigns**: targeted messages to dormant or lapsed users.
47. **Founder welcome note**: a short personal first email from the founder.
48. **Behavior-aware capture**: signup prompts triggered by exit intent or scroll depth.
49. **Regular newsletter**: a steady send that keeps the brand in mind.
50. **Deliverability work**: sender authentication and list cleaning so mail reaches the inbox.
51. **Onboarding sequence**: timed emails that lead new users to their first success.
52. **Win-back series**: give departed customers a solid reason to return.
53. **Expired-trial follow-up**: trials that ran out can still convert with the right nudge.

## Partners and programs (54-64)

54. **Affiliate scouting through links**: see who links to rivals and invite those sites.
55. **Ads via creators' accounts**: run paid ads from an influencer's handle for more trusted reach.
56. **Reseller scheme**: let agencies sell your product, perhaps under their own label.
57. **Certified expert network**: trained partners who set up and support your product.
58. **Newsletter swaps**: trade mentions with a neighbouring, non-competing newsletter.
59. **Expert quotes for reporters**: answer journalist requests through source-matching services.
60. **Shared retargeting audiences**: swap audience pools with a complementary company, only where visitors consented to that use and the ad platform's rules allow uploaded lists; in the EU this needs a lawful basis, so ask the owner to check privacy rules first.
61. **Shared chat channels**: a joint channel with a partner or key customer.
62. **Affiliate program**: pay commission to people who send buyers.
63. **Integration co-marketing**: launch and promote with the tools you connect to.
64. **Community sponsorships**: fund a forum, newsletter or publication your buyers read.

## Events (65-72)

65. **Live webinars**: teach something and collect leads at the same time.
66. **Virtual summits**: many speakers online, each bringing their own audience.
67. **Roadshow**: travel to meet customers face to face.
68. **Hosting local meetups**: gather people in the cities that matter to you.
69. **Meetup sponsorship**: pay for a local group's pizza and visibility.
70. **Conference talks**: speak where your buyers gather.
71. **Your own conference**: become the hub of your niche.
72. **Conference sponsorship**: a booth or logo for brand visibility.

## Press and media (73-76)

73. **Buying media**: acquire a newsletter, show or site in your field.
74. **Pitched stories**: offer newsworthy angles to relevant outlets.
75. **Funding news**: use a raise announcement as a press hook.
76. **Documentary content**: film the story of your industry or customers.

## Launches and promotions (77-86)

77. **Black Friday deals**: yearly sale for a spike in urgency and signups.
78. **Launch-site debut**: a prepared Product Hunt launch for early adopters.
79. **Referral queue-jumping**: let people move up the early-access line by referring friends.
80. **New Year offers**: ride fresh budgets and resolutions.
81. **Early-bird pricing**: discounted first tiers for those who join at launch.
82. **Other launch directories**: list on BetaList, Launching Next, AlternativeTo and similar; directories close or change terms, so confirm each one is still active and free before you plan around it.
83. **Follow-to-win X giveaways**: prizes tied to follows or reposts; read the platform's contest rules first.
84. **Targeted giveaways**: prizes your ideal buyer wants, with email entry.
85. **Grand-prize giveaways**: a trip or similar big prize draws large attention.

Giveaways (83-85) fall under platform contest rules and national prize-draw or gambling law; the owner checks both before any is run.
86. **Lifetime deals**: one payment now brings cash and users, but cap them.
