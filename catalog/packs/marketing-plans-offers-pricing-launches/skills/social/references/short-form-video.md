# Short-form video: structures, hooks, scripts

Applies to TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Timings and thresholds are working numbers; confirm platform specs in current help pages.

## Three structures

Problem then fix (15 to 30 s)
- 0 to 3 s: name the problem. 3 to 10 s: why it hurts. 10 to 25 s: the fix. 25 to 30 s: next step.

List (30 to 60 s)
- 0 to 3 s: "[N] ways to [result]". 3 to 50 s: one item every 5 to 8 s. 50 to 60 s: next step.

Walkthrough (30 to 60 s)
- 0 to 3 s: show the finished result. 3 to 8 s: "here is how". 8 to 50 s: quick clear steps. 50 to 60 s: result and next step.

Story arc (45 to 60 s): tease the outcome 0 to 3 s; context and stakes to 15 s; what happened to 45 s; the result to 55 s; lesson and next step to 60 s. Suits personal stories, cases, testimonials.

Point-of-view skit (15 to 30 s): on-screen text sets the scene in 3 s; act the relatable moment to 25 s; end on a twist. Suits humour and niche communities.

## Captions on screen
Many viewers watch with the sound off, so captions help them follow. Show at most 2 lines, 3 to 5 words each, in a bold sans-serif with a dark outline, colour the key words, match the speech timing exactly. Free and paid editors both do this.

## Ideas by business
- Software: show the result first, before and after, "watch me do [task] in [N] seconds".
- Shop: unboxing, side-by-side comparisons, how it is made, buyer reviews (real ones only).
- Services: reveal the process, client transformations, myth busting.
- Personal brand: lessons, opinions, a day in the life.

## Hook bank
Curiosity: "The part of [tool] nobody told you about"; "I tried [thing] for 21 days and did not expect this"; "Why does nobody mention this?"; "Is it just me or did everyone miss this?"; "The reason [common thing] fails is..."
Value: "How to [result] in [time]"; "[N] [things] that [benefit]"; "[Topic] in 60 seconds"; "A quicker way to [result]"; "Stuck with [problem]? Watch this."
Warning: "Quit [habit], here is why"; "[N] mistakes dragging down your [metric]"; "Why [popular thing] is quietly hurting you".
Story: "Three months ago I was [state]. Now I am [state]."; "I used to believe [X]. Then [event]."; "I got [topic] badly wrong"; "Let me tell you about the time I [situation]".
Debate: "Unpopular opinion: [claim]"; "[Advice] is actually wrong"; "This will annoy people, but..."; "Most [audience] get this backwards".
Only make claims you can back up.

## Script template
```
Title: [working title]
Platform / length / format (talking head, slides, demo, screen recording)

Hook, 0 to 3 s:  what is seen | what is heard | text on screen
Body: timestamp and beat, one line each
Closing, last 3 to 5 s: spoken line | text | action (follow, comment, link in bio)
Production: sound choice | clips needed | graphics
```

## Visual styles
- Talking head: window or ring light, eyes on the lens, hand gestures, a background with some interest.
- Slides: 2 to 4 s per slide, key point as text, one visual style, voiceover or sound.
- Screen recording: zoom on the key area, highlight the cursor, smooth deliberate moves, optional face in a corner.
- B-roll: show, do not tell; 1 to 3 s cuts on the voiceover beats; mix wide, medium, close.

## Sound
- Trending sound: for entertainment or lifestyle clips where it fits, while the trend is rising; skip it if it fights your message or is fading.
- Your own audio: teaching, story times, demos, building a recognisable voice.
- Voiceover: a touch faster than chat, vary pitch, pause on the key line, record in a quiet room and denoise. Synthetic voices are acceptable for faceless clips (disclose where a platform requires it).
- Music: energy matches content; use the platform's licensed library, not commercial tracks; duck it under speech.

## Posting
Find your posting times from your own analytics, then test around them.

The rhythm below is the skill's own starting point, not a measured value.

| Aim | Minimum | Better |
|---|---|---|
| Growing | 1 per day | 2 to 4 per day |
| Holding | 3 per week | 1 per day |
| Testing | 2 per week | 5 per week |

Batch day: ideas 30 min (10 to 20); scripts 1 h (5 to 10); filming 2 h; editing and captions 2 to 3 h; scheduling 30 min. Posting itself needs the owner's word.

## Measure and adjust
Watch-time share (does it hold interest), completion rate (did hook and body deliver), saves (worth revisiting), shares (worth passing on), comments (sparked talk), follows (wanted more).
Test one thing at a time: the opener, the length (15, 30, 60 s), the format, the time of day, the call to action.
Pivot signals: several clips in a row with completion well below your own usual level means change the hooks; many views but few follows means check the call to action and audience match; many saves but few shares means useful but not social; many negative comments means adjust tone or own the debate on purpose.

## Mistakes to avoid
Slow openings; no text overlay; poor sound (it ends watching fast); length beyond what the idea needs; no call to action; ignoring comments in the first hour.
