# Section patterns

Skeletons to adapt. Fill every bracket from the rival record, never from memory.

## Summary block

Start the page with: [Rival] is strong at [strength] and weaker at [gap]. [Product] is built around [focus] and offers [difference]. Pick [Rival] when [their best case]; pick [Product] when [yours].

## Dimension paragraph

Describe how the rival handles the topic and who that works well for, then the limit that bites for a certain user. Describe your own approach, what it buys, and the honest cost. Close with the kind of team that tends to prefer each.

## Feature section, per category

For each side: two or three sentences on the approach, then strengths and limits as short lists. End with a bottom line: choose [A] if [situation], choose [B] if [situation].

## Pricing section

A table with rows for free tier, entry price, middle tier, top tier, enterprise; columns for the rival and you. Below it:
- What each named plan includes.
- Extra costs: add-ons, setup, paid support, usage overages.
- A worked yearly total for one sample team size, with the arithmetic shown (for instance 6 seats at a listed monthly price times 12) and the date the prices were read.

## Service and support table

Rows: documentation, response time (only if published), channels, onboarding, dedicated success contact and the tier that includes it. Add a line on what public reviews say about support, naming the review source and date.

## Who it suits

For each product: a short list of use cases, team sizes, workflows and budgets that fit, then one sentence describing the ideal customer.

## Migration

- What moves cleanly and with what caveats.
- What must be rebuilt and how much work.
- Help you offer: import tool or hands-on service, guide, expected duration, support during the move.
- A switcher quote with name, role and company, used only with permission.

## Switcher proof

Quotes that state why the person left and what changed, then two or three concrete results with the company and the measure. Use only results you can substantiate.

## Tables

- Replace tick marks with a short phrase of substance per cell ("full support, includes X" against "basic, no Y").
- Group rows by theme: core function, teamwork, integrations, security, support.
- Ratings are fine when each has a one-line reason beside it.
