# Platform profiles

Rhythm and length numbers here are the skill's own starting points, not measured values. Posting times and audience profiles are deliberately not given: read them from your own analytics. Platforms change rhythm, ranking and features without notice, so check each platform's current guidance and your own analytics before committing.

At a glance:

| Platform | Fit | Rhythm | Lead format |
|---|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | B2B, expertise, hiring | 3 to 5 per week | Carousels, stories |
| Twitter/X | Tech, news, community | 3 to 10 per day, replies included | Threads, opinions |
| Instagram | Visual and lifestyle brands | 1 to 2 posts plus Stories daily | Reels, carousels |
| TikTok | Awareness, younger viewers | 1 to 4 per day | Short video |
| Facebook | Communities, local business | 1 to 2 per day | Groups, native video |

## LinkedIn
- Audience: professionals, decision makers, job seekers.
- Works: personal stories with a work lesson, opinions against the grain, building in the open, original data, document carousels, polls that start a debate.
- Fails: heavy promotion, recycled quotes, corporate tone with no person in it, links in the post body.
- Format: the first line must earn the "see more" click; short paragraphs; keep it as short as the idea allows; link in the first comment; tag only people with a real reason.
- Ranking habits: the first hour counts most; comments outrank reactions, reactions outrank clicks; time spent reading is a quality signal; carousels reach well; polls get replies but build little authority.

## Twitter/X
- Audience: tech-minded, news-driven, niche circles. Times vary; test.
- Works: strong opinions, teaching threads, backstage moments, replying to others, humour that fits the brand, live commentary.
- Fails: pure promotion, threads with a weak opening, leaving replies unanswered, scheduling everything so you are never present.
- Format: short posts are worth testing against longer ones; thread = hook in post 1, promise, deliver; a quote post with your own point beats a plain repost; images and video stop the scroll.
- Ranking habits: replies and quotes build standing; threads keep people on the platform; the first 30 minutes matter; a paid tier may lift reach.

## Instagram
- Audience: visual-first.
- Works: sharp visuals, backstage Stories, Reels, carousels that teach, customer-made content, interactive Stories (polls, questions).
- Fails: weak images, walls of text on images, skipping Stories and Reels, nothing but promotion.
- Format: compare Reels with static posts in your own analytics; the first frame must hook; carousels up to 10 slides of teaching; use the sticker features.
- Ranking habits: Reels favoured; saves and shares beat likes; Stories keep you near the top of followers' feeds; steady beats perfect.

## TikTok
- Audience: there to be entertained.
- Works: unpolished native clips, rising sounds and formats, teaching wrapped in entertainment, point-of-view and day-in-the-life clips, video replies to comments, duets and stitches.
- Fails: over-produced clips, ignoring trends, hard selling, horizontal footage reused.
- Format: hook within 1 to 2 seconds; start under 30 seconds; vertical 9:16; consistent posting helps the system learn you.

## Facebook
- Audience: community-minded.
- Works: Groups, native and live video, local news and events, questions that start a discussion.
- Fails: links off the platform, promotion only, unanswered comments, cross-posting without adapting.
