# Affiliate programs

## Commission models

- Percentage of sale: fits shops and SaaS with clear prices. Example wording: "earn a share of every sale you send".
- Flat fee per action: fits lead generation, trials, freemium. Example: a fixed amount per qualified demo.
- Recurring commission: fits subscriptions; a share of revenue for a fixed number of months.
- Tiered: the rate rises with volume, which motivates top performers. Example: three bands by sales count.

Percent and amount levels are the user's decision. No typical ranges are given here, because none are sourced. Always work out cost per sale and total exposure, and get approval before publishing rates.

## Cookie window

How long after a click the affiliate still gets credit. No typical length is given here. Measure your own: count the days between each buyer's first tracked click and their purchase, over your last few months of sales, and pick a window that covers most of your own buyers. Shorter fits impulse purchases and longer fits long sales cycles; no expiry only for premium partners by agreement.

## Finding affiliates

- Customers who already make content
- Reviewers and bloggers in your field
- Video creators in the niche
- Newsletter writers
- Companies with complementary tools
- Consultants and agencies

    [sender]

## Enablement kit

Give each affiliate: tracking link or code; product summary and benefits; who the product is for; comparison with alternatives; logos and banners; sample copy and talking points; case studies; a demo or free account; FAQ with answers to objections; payment terms and schedule.
