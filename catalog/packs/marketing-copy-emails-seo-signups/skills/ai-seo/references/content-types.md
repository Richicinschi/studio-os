# Tactics by page type

Useful for ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Copilot; harmless for Google's AI features.

## Software product pages
Aim: appear for "what is [category]" and "best [category]".
- Open with one paragraph: what the product does and for whom.
- Compare to the category as a whole, not just to named rivals.
- Swap adjectives for facts you can measure, such as a median page-render time in milliseconds.
- Back claims with customer numbers.
- Show plans and prices on the page, and publish a plain-text price file next to it (see machine-files).
- Answer real buyer questions in an FAQ.

## Blog posts
Aim: be the source an AI quotes on your subject.
- One query per post; make the heading echo it.
- Define the term in the opening paragraph when the query is "what is".
- Include your own data, research or quotes from named experts.
- Print the last-updated date; add an author bio with relevant credentials.
- Link inward to related product and feature pages.

## Comparison and alternative pages
Aim: appear for "X vs Y" and "best X alternatives".
- Tables with named criteria and scores, not paragraphs alone.
- Even-handed wording; slanted comparisons get discounted.
- Fresh prices and feature lists.
- The competitors skill covers building these.

## Docs and help articles
Aim: appear for "how to do [task] with [product]".
- Numbered steps, stated prerequisites, stated outcome.
- Code samples when they fit, HowTo markup, screenshots with meaningful alt text.

## Local business and online shops
Google's AI features read product feeds and business listings for these queries.
- Keep Merchant Center feeds accurate for stock, price and attributes.
- Fill in the Business Profile fully: hours, services, photos, posts, answered questions.
- Collect recent reviews in good volume and reply to them.
- Add service-area markup for local services.
- Use Business Agent where available for chat-style customer help.
