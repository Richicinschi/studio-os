# Launch checklist

Trim to the stage and channels you chose. Items that mean sending or posting are prepared, not executed, until the owner agrees.

## Before launch
- [ ] Landing page states the value in one sentence
- [ ] Email capture or waitlist form works
- [ ] Early-access list exists
- [ ] Owned channels are set up (email, blog, community)
- [ ] Profiles on chosen rented platforms are tidy
- [ ] Borrowed opportunities are listed with a contact for each
- [ ] Directory listing drafted, if used
- [ ] Assets ready: screenshots, demo video, clips
- [ ] Onboarding flow tested end to end
- [ ] Analytics and conversion tracking confirmed

## Launch day
- [ ] Announcement email ready for the list
- [ ] Blog post ready
- [ ] Social posts written and timed
- [ ] Directory listing ready to go live
- [ ] In-app announcement for current users
- [ ] Site banner or notice prepared
- [ ] Someone assigned to reply to comments and messages
- [ ] Someone watching for bugs and feedback

## After launch
- [ ] Onboarding email series running
- [ ] Follow-ups to engaged leads
- [ ] Next roundup email mentions the launch
- [ ] Comparison pages live
- [ ] Interactive demo built
- [ ] Feedback gathered and acted on
- [ ] Next launch moment scheduled
