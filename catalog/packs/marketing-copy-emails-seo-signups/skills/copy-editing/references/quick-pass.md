# Quick-pass checks

For a short text or a fast review where seven full passes would be too much. Go from words up to paragraphs.

## Words

Cut:
- weak intensifiers: very, really, extremely, incredibly
- filler: just, actually, basically
- "in order to" (write "to")
- "that" where the sentence works without it
- empty nouns: things, stuff

Swap puffed-up words for plain ones:
- utilize, leverage: use
- implement: set up
- facilitate: help
- innovative, cutting-edge: new, modern
- robust: strong
- seamless: smooth

Also watch:
- adverbs, most of which can go
- passive voice: make the doer the subject
- verbs turned into nouns: "make a decision" is "decide", "give consideration to" is "consider"

A longer swap list sits in [plain words](plain-words.md).

## Sentences

- One idea each.
- Mix short and long so the rhythm does not drone.
- Put the important part first.
- No more than three conjunctions in one sentence.
- Aim for about 25 words or fewer (a starting point, not a measured value); go over only on purpose.

## Paragraphs

- One topic each.
- On the web, 2 to 4 sentences (a starting point, not a measured value).
- The first sentence should earn the second.
- Paragraphs follow each other logically.
- Leave white space so the page can be skimmed.
