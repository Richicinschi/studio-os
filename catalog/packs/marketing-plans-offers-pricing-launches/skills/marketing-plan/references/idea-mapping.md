# Idea bank: mapping the 139 ideas to funnel stages

Section 12 of the plan lists every idea in the marketing-ideas library (numbered 1 to 139) with a stage and a status for this client. The numbers below must match that skill's numbering exactly. If the library grows, give each new idea a primary stage by the rules in funnel-stages.md and extend these lists.

Five ideas serve two stages and can appear under both: the product-competition idea (117), the data-driven "moneyball" method (114), upsells inside the app (91), lifetime deals (86), and early-access referrals (79). That is why the bank holds 144 entries for 139 ideas; the stage headline counts are entries, not unique ideas.

## Applying it to a client

1. **Category filter.** Drop ideas that do not fit the product type (importers for non-software, extensions for non-browser products, developer relations for non-developer tools). Keep a one-line reason.
2. **Voice filter.** Send ideas that clash with the voice to the skip list with the reason. Typical clashes: lifetime deals with premium positioning; social giveaways, humor, celebrity-video and reality-TV tactics with serious, clinical or luxury voices.
3. **Timing status.** Use the default above, then set Now in Q1 (already in the 90-day plan or runnable with no new capacity), Q2 (after the foundation), Q3+ (after funding or release), Q4+ (long game). An unusual asset shifts timing earlier, for example a famous founder makes speaking more valuable.
4. **Client note.** One line for every idea marked Now, Q2 or Q3+, tied to a named asset, competitor or goal of this client. Example for a fictional kennel-booking tool: "comparison pages against the three biggest booking apps, since owners search by those names."
5. **Summary block.** Count ideas per stage (acquisition is usually largest early on), the cross-cutting count, and the skipped count with the reason class. State the share of the whole tactical surface the plan covers (a fraction, not everything, and that is correct for the stage) and note that as capacity grows the bank becomes the inventory for scaling without losing coherence.

Do not pad the bank with ideas that obviously do not fit just to reach 139; the skip list with reasons is part of completeness.
