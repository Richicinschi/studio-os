# Free plans, trials and large accounts

Figures are the skill's own starting points, not measured values. Prices are invented examples.

## Free plan (freemium)

Fits when:
- the product spreads through use or network effects,
- free users add something (content, data, referrals),
- the market is big enough that a small share converting still gives volume,
- serving a free user costs almost nothing,
- a clear limit gives people a reason to upgrade.

Risks: many never pay, the product can look cheap, free users still cost support, and raising prices later gets harder.

## Free trial

Fits when:
- value takes time to see,
- setup effort is needed,
- several people decide the purchase,
- the price is high,
- the product sticks once configured.

Good practice:
- Length: a starting point is 7-14 days for simple products and 14-30 days for complex ones; check how long your own users take to reach first value and set the trial just past that.
- Give full access, not a cut-down version.
- Show a countdown and send reminders.
- Card upfront or not is a trade: asking for a card usually means fewer people start but those who do are better qualified. Measure your own trial-to-paid rate for each variant over the same period, counting trials started and trials that became paid.

## Hybrids

- Free plan plus a time-limited taste of the paid features.
- Reverse trial: full access first, then a drop to the free plan. People feel the premium value, then live with limits until they decide to pay.

## Large accounts

Add a "contact sales" plan when:
- deals pass a recurring-revenue level where a sales conversation pays for itself (work out your own threshold from the cost of a sales call),
- buyers want their own contract,
- onboarding or implementation is needed,
- security or compliance reviews apply,
- procurement is involved.

Expected basics: single sign-on, audit logs, admin controls, an uptime commitment, security certifications. Extras that justify the price: a named support contact, custom onboarding, training, custom integrations, a say in the roadmap.

Pricing shapes (invented numbers):
- Volume per seat: 14 per seat as standard, 9 per seat above 100 seats.
- Platform fee plus usage: 400 per month base plus a small charge per call above an included amount.
- Outcome-linked: a share of transactions or revenue the product helps produce.
