# Paywall layouts

Three layout sketches. Brackets are placeholders you fill from the real product. Replace every price with the real, current one.

## Locked feature

```
[padlock]  Part of the Plus plan
[preview of the locked result]
[Feature] helps you [outcome]
  - [what it does]
  - [what it does]
[Get Plus, [price] per month]
[Not now]
```

Keep the preview honest: show the real output, not a mock-up that overpromises.

## Cap reached

```
Your free space is full
[meter at 100 percent]
Free: [n] items   Plus: no cap
[See Plus]   [Remove an item]
```

Always give a free way forward (remove, archive, wait for reset) next to the upgrade.

## Trial ending

```
[n] days left in your trial
You would lose access to:
  - [feature you used]
  - [content you made]
So far you have:
  - [concrete result with a number]
[Keep Plus]
[Remind me later]   [Switch to free]
```

State what happens to the user's data after expiry. The switch-to-free choice must be as easy to see as the keep button.
