# Playbooks for material the user already has

## Interview and sales call transcripts
Pull out pains, triggers, wanted outcomes, wording, objections and the alternatives named. The key moment is the point where the person decided to look for help: what had they tried, and what would "fixed" look like to them. Mark who said it (role, segment) and whether they volunteered it or answered a prompt.

## Surveys
Split answers by plan, use case or tenure before reading totals. Compare free-text answers with the closed questions, since they often disagree, and report the disagreement rather than choosing a side. Read all of the open answers once, then pick the small share that holds specific, concrete detail and work from those.

## Support conversations
Sort tickets into groups before counting: defect, confusion, missing capability, expectation mismatch. Look for repeat complaints, places where users get lost, requests, and phrases like "I wish it could". Equal weight for all tickets hides the signal.

## Win/loss notes and churn notes
Wins: what tipped the choice, and which rival nearly won. Losses and churn: label the main cause (price, features, fit, timing, other) and analyse each cause separately. Never average across causes.

## NPS comments
Passives and detractors teach more about what to fix than promoters. Always read the score with its comment. A 9 with a specific complaint is worth more than a 10 with no words.

## Synthesis report skeleton
Top themes ranked by how often and how strongly they appear. For each: name; one or two line summary; "seen in X of Y sources"; intensity (high, medium, low, based on the emotion in the wording); two or three quotes with source and date; implication. Close with contradictions and open questions.
