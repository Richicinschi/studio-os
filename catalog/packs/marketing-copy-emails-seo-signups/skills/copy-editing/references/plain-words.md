# Plain words

When a formal word has a short everyday equivalent, use the short one. Grouped by pattern so the habit is easy to learn.

## Long verbs, short verbs

- utilise, employ, operate: use, work
- commence, initiate: start, begin
- terminate, cease, discontinue: end, stop
- purchase, procure, acquire, obtain: buy, get
- assist, facilitate: help
- request, enquire: ask
- notify, inform, advise: tell
- demonstrate, indicate, illustrate: show
- locate, ascertain: find, find out
- attempt, endeavour: try
- require: need
- retain, preserve: keep
- provide, furnish, issue: give, send
- forward, transmit, despatch: send
- participate: take part
- accomplish, finalise: do, finish
- anticipate, envisage: expect
- enable, permit, authorise: let, allow
- reduce, diminish, alleviate: cut, ease
- expedite, accelerate: speed up
- modify: change
- peruse: read
- consult, liaise with: ask, work with

## Long phrases, short phrases

- in order to, with a view to, for the purpose of: to
- due to the fact that, owing to, in view of the fact that, on the grounds that: because
- prior to: before
- subsequent to: after
- at the present time, currently: now (or drop it)
- in the event of, provided that: if
- in excess of: more than
- in the majority of instances: usually
- in the near future: soon
- in addition to, furthermore, moreover: also, and
- with regard to, with reference to, in relation to, relating to, concerning, regarding: about
- on behalf of: for
- in accordance with, pursuant to: under
- by means of: by
- a large number of, numerous: many
- sufficient: enough
- additional, supplementary: extra, more
- approximately: about
- nevertheless: but
- consequently, accordingly, thus: so
- thereafter: then
- to date: so far

## Cut these altogether

Single words that rarely earn their place: absolutely, actually, basically, obviously, quite, really, very. Padding phrases, delete on sight:
- "the fact of the matter is"
- "to all intents and purposes"
- "it should be understood"
- "in the final analysis"
- "last but not least"
- "all things being equal"
- "at this moment in time"
- "a total of"
- "of course"
- "at the end of the day"
- "as a matter of fact"

Two with a swap: Replace "I am of the opinion that" with "I think", and "in due course" with "soon" or a date.
