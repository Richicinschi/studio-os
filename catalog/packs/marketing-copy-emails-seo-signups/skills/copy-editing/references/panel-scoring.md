# Panel scoring

A second quality gate after the seven passes. One reader misses things that several different readers catch, so for important copy simulate a small panel.

## Procedure

1. Choose 3 to 5 personas that fit the copy type (panels below).
2. Each persona scores the copy from 1 to 10 within their own expertise.
3. Each also writes concrete critique: what to change, not only a number.
4. Revise, starting with the lowest score.
5. Score again. Stop when every persona gives 7 or more and the average is at least 8.

The panel is a thinking device: you play the personas, and say so. Do not present the scores as real research.

## Panels

Landing page:
- conversion copywriter: clarity, strength of the call to action, order of benefits
- UX writer: scannability, mental load, flow through the page
- target customer: speaks to them, earns their trust
- brand strategist: voice consistency, accurate positioning

Email sequence:
- email specialist: subject lines, opens and clicks
- copywriter: hooks, story, persuasion
- deliverability analyst: spam-filter red flags and trigger words
- target customer: relevance, value, reason to unsubscribe

Sales page or long form:
- direct-response copywriter: offer shape, objection handling, urgency
- skeptical buyer: gaps in proof, trust problems, red flags
- editor: flow, readability, brevity
- SEO specialist: keyword coverage, match with search intent

## Rubric

- 9 to 10: ready to publish
- 7 to 8: strong, small tweaks only
- 5 to 6: works but has clear gaps, another pass needed
- 3 to 4: serious problems, major revision
- 1 to 2: broken at the root, rethink the approach

## When to run it

- Always: launch copy, pricing pages, landing pages with heavy traffic.
- Recommended: email sequences, sales pages, ad copy.
- Optional: blog posts, social posts, internal documents.
- Skip: quick updates, small edits, low-stakes text.
