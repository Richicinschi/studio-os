## When to use

Typical wordings: "give me ideas to get users", "I have no idea how to promote this", "what else can I try", "brainstorm ways to grow", "which tactics suit a tiny budget". This skill is the starting point when the person is stuck or wants a menu to choose from. It finds a few fitting ideas and sketches how to begin. It does not run the channel.

Where the neighbours take over:
- A full plan with stages, owners and a calendar: marketing-plan. It also maps the ideas here onto the funnel stages.
- Scaling search pages (idea 4): programmatic-seo.
- Versus pages (idea 11): competitors.
- Email programs: emails.
- Free utilities as a lead source (idea 15): lead-magnets.
- Viral and referral mechanics (idea 93): referrals.
- Ad, social or copy execution: ad-creative, social or copywriting, whichever matches the channel.

## How it works

1. Look for a saved product marketing context file (check `.agents/product-marketing.md`, then the older `product-marketing-context.md`). Read it first and ask only what it does not answer.
2. Ask, in one short message, what is still unknown:
   - What the product is and who it is for, if the context file does not say.
   - Stage and the single growth goal right now (first 50 users, more trials, more revenue).
   - Budget in money and hours per week, and team size.
   - What was already tried and how it went.
   - Which competitor moves the person envies.
3. Pick the matching list from the reference files, which hold all 139 ideas, numbered and grouped:
   - [Content, search, tools, ads, social](marketing-ideas/content-tools-ads-social.md): ideas 1 to 44.
   - [Email, partners, events, press, launches](marketing-ideas/email-partners-events-launches.md): ideas 45 to 86.
   - [Product-led, formats, stunts, platforms, abroad, audience](marketing-ideas/product-formats-platforms-audience.md): ideas 87 to 139.
4. Narrow to 3 to 5 ideas using the filters below. Put free routes first; if an idea costs money, say how much before recommending it. Never send, post or buy anything for the person without a clear yes.
5. For each pick, write:
   - **Idea**: its name and one line.
   - **Why it fits**: the link to their stage, audience and constraints.
   - **First steps**: the first 2 or 3 actions.
   - **What good looks like**: a measurable sign it works, such as signups per week.
   - **Cost**: hours, money and skills needed.
6. When they choose, go deeper on only the chosen ideas: a week-by-week start, what to measure, and which neighbour skill to open next.

## Checks before done

- Context file read, and no question asked that it already answered.
- 3 to 5 ideas, each with all five fields, each tied to the stated stage and budget.
- Free options offered before paid ones; every cost named.
- Idea numbers match the reference files.
- Nothing was sent, posted or purchased.
- The person knows which neighbour skill to open for the idea they pick.
