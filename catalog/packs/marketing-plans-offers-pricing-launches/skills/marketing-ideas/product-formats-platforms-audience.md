# Ideas 87 to 139: product-led, formats, stunts, platforms, abroad, audience

## Product-led growth (87-96)

87. **"Powered by" badge**: a small mark on customers' pages that gives free exposure.
88. **Free migration help**: you move the data from the old tool for them.
89. **Contract buyouts**: pay the exit fee on a rival's contract.
90. **One-click signup**: social sign-in to cut form friction.
91. **In-app upgrade prompts**: show the paid option at the moment it helps.
92. **Newsletter referral programs**: reader rewards built into a newsletter.
93. **Viral loops**: product mechanics where using it invites others.
94. **Cancellation flow design**: save the customer or at least learn why they leave.
95. **Concierge setup**: hands-on onboarding for large accounts.
96. **Continuous onboarding tuning**: keep improving the first-run experience.

## Content formats (97-109)

97. **Music playlists**: curated lists for your audience's work or mood.
98. **Free templates**: ready-to-use files people can start with today.
99. **Comic-style stories**: explain a complex idea as a visual narrative.
100. **Promo video**: a well-made film showing the product.
101. **Interview series**: talk with customers, experts and leaders.
102. **Screenshot-ready social proof**: designed templates for sharing praise.
103. **Online courses**: teaching that builds authority and gathers leads.
104. **A book**: author one to claim expertise.
105. **Annual report**: yearly data and trends for your industry.
106. **Year-end recaps**: personal summaries users like to post.
107. **Your own podcast**: reach people on the commute.
108. **Public changelog**: shows steady progress.
109. **Open demos**: live sessions showing real use.

## Unconventional (110-122)

110. **Industry awards**: be the one who hands out the trophies.
111. **Public challenges**: a contest or streak that spreads by itself.
112. **Docuseries about customers**: reality-style episodes following real users.
113. **Taking a stand**: position against a common practice in your field.
114. **Undervalued-channel hunting**: use data to find cheap attention others overlook.
115. **Curated resource lists**: collect the best links for your audience.
116. **Grants**: give money to customers or community members.
117. **Sponsored competitions**: prizes for the best thing made with your product.
118. **Celebrity video messages**: personalised clips bought from a talent platform; such platforms usually license standard clips for personal use only, so book the business licence tier, check its current terms and label the clip as paid promotion.
119. **Out-of-home ads**: billboards and transit posters.
120. **Stunts**: one bold, memorable moment.
121. **Guerrilla tactics**: cheap, surprising actions in unexpected places.
122. **Humor**: be funny to be remembered.

## Platforms and marketplaces (123-130)

123. **Open-source parts**: release components or tools to earn developer goodwill.
124. **Store listing optimisation**: tune app-store titles, screenshots and keywords.
125. **Ecosystem marketplaces**: list on the app stores of big platforms such as CRM or shop systems.
126. **Creator reviews**: get video reviewers to try your product.
127. **Your own video channel**: tutorials and viewpoints on a long-term schedule.
128. **Software directories**: submit to comparison and listing sites.
129. **Review-site upkeep**: collect and answer ratings on review platforms.
130. **Live audio rooms**: host live talks on a platform that still offers them (LinkedIn retired its standalone audio events in 2024, so check what each network offers now).

## Abroad (131-132)

131. **New countries**: enter foreign markets with proper localisation.
132. **Local pricing**: set prices to match purchasing power in each place.

## Developer and technical (133-136)

133. **Investor outreach**: ask investors to introduce you to their portfolio companies.
134. **Certification programs**: credentials that prove skill with your product.
135. **Support as marketing**: service so good that customers tell the story.
136. **Developer relations**: build ties with developer communities.

## Audience-specific (137-139)

137. **Two-sided referral rewards**: both the inviter and the invitee gain.
138. **Podcast guest tour**: appear on many shows your audience listens to.
139. **Customer wording**: copy the phrases your customers actually use.
