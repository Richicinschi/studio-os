# Hooks and post skeletons

The opening line decides whether the rest gets read. Fill the brackets with your own facts; never invent results or quotes.

## Hook bank

Curiosity
- "I had [belief] backwards."
- "What actually causes [outcome] is not what people say."
- "[Good result] in just [short time]."
- "Nobody mentions [inside detail]."

Story
- "Last month, [odd event] happened."
- "I came close to [costly error]."
- "In [year] I was [old state]. Now I am [new state]."
- "[Person] said one thing that stuck with me."

Value
- "How to [outcome] without [usual pain]:"
- "[Number] [items] that [result]:"
- "The plainest route to [outcome]:"
- "Quit [habit]. Try this."

Contrarian
- "Hot take: [claim]"
- "The usual advice on [topic] fails. Reason:"
- "I dropped [habit] and [result]."
- "People say [X]. In practice it is [Y]."

Proof
- "[Result] in [timeframe]. What happened:"
- "[Number] readers asked about [topic]. My answer:"
- "[Mentor] taught me [lesson]."

## LinkedIn skeletons

Story post: hook with the surprising outcome; where and when; the problem; what you tried and what followed; the turn; the result; the lesson; one question to the reader.

Opinion post: the claim in one line; "My reasons:"; three short reasons; what to do instead; invite pushback.

List post: "[N] things I learned about [topic] from [credential]"; numbered points, each with one line of explanation; one closing insight; ask which point resonated.

How-to post: "How to [outcome] in [time]"; steps 1 to 3, each with an arrow line giving the reason, the key detail or the common slip; the result to expect; a question or next step.

## X thread skeletons

Teaching thread: post 1 hook and promise ("Here is how to [outcome], step by step"); posts 2 to 7 one step each; last post recap and an invitation to follow for more on the topic.

Story thread: post 1 intriguing opener; posts 2 to 6 beats that build tension; post 7 resolution and lesson; last post takeaway and a question.

Breakdown thread: post 1 "[Company or person] just [did X]. Why it works and what to borrow:"; posts 2 to 6 points; post 7 your main takeaway; last post a related thought.

## Instagram skeletons

Carousel: slide 1 bold statement or question; slides 2 to 9 one idea each with visual and text; slide 10 recap and call to action. Caption expands the topic and repeats the call to action.

Reel: hook 0 to 2 s (pattern break or bold claim); setup 2 to 5 s; payload 5 to 25 s; call to action 25 to 30 s (follow, comment, share, tap through).
