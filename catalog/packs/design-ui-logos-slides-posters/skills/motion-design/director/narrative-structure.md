# Narrative structure

A move can be told as four short acts. Start with how much time each gets, then what happens in it.

## Time per act

| Total length | Anticipation | Action | Reaction | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100-200 ms | none | 60-70% | none | 30-40% |
| 200-400 ms | 10-15% | 40-50% | 10-15% | 25-30% |
| 400-800 ms | 15-20% | 30-40% | 15-20% | 20-25% |
| 800 ms and up | 20% | 30-35% | 15-20% | 25-30% |

In general terms: anticipation 10-20%, action 30-50%, reaction 10-20%, resolution 20-30%.

## What each act contains

**Anticipation.** A wind-up step against the coming direction, elements gathering before they spread, the context dimming, a held pose of about 50 ms for tension. Leave it out for anything under 150 ms and for hovers.

**Action.** The peak, and the part that actually says something. Choose its flavour by intent:
- Alert: fast, direct, sharp easing.
- Transition: smooth and flowing, gentle curves.
- Celebration: explosive and expanding, exponential curves, radial.
- Data chart: controlled and precise, close to linear.

**Reaction.** The world answers: shadows adjust 50-100 ms after the main move, neighbours shift 50-150 ms after, wider surroundings ripple 100-200 ms after, and counter-motion happens alongside. Omit this act for plain toggles and checkboxes.

**Resolution.** Settle with overshoot (a spring or back-easing) and let opacity finish with ease-out. Leave 100-200 ms of quiet before the next movement. Even 50 ms of settling changes how it feels.

## How personalities play the acts

| Personality | Anticipation | Action | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| Playful | big wind-up | bouncy, overshooting | wobbly settle |
| Premium | faint tension | smooth, controlled | graceful ease |
| Corporate | tiny or none | direct, efficient | clean stop |
| Energetic | quick gather | explosive | fast snap |

## Familiar arcs

- Reveal: tension (dim, scale to 95%), emerge (100%, opaque), surroundings adjust, rest.
- Departure: gather (98%), leave, the gap closes, the layout settles.
- Transformation: destabilise with a shake, morph, a secondary detail appears, the new state breathes.
- Celebration: compress for 50-100 ms, burst with scale and particles, settle, end calm in a positive state.

## Joining beats into longer stories

Beats can overlap (fluid), run one after another (clear) or run together (parallel). Shapes of progression:
- Build-up: low, rising, peak, settling.
- Cycle: leave, peak, come back, repeat.
- Impact: a sudden hit, a ripple, a slow settle.
