# Persona template

Fill only what the evidence supports. A blank field is better than a guess.

- Name and role (a plain role label; a cute invented name is usually a distraction)
- Profile: title range, company size and stage, industry if narrow, who they report to, team size
- Main job to be done, one sentence
- Trigger events that start the search
- Top three pains, in their words where possible
- Desired outcomes: what success looks like, how they measure it, how it looks to their boss or team
- Objections and fears that stall buying or switching
- Alternatives considered: rivals, DIY, doing nothing, hiring
- Vocabulary: phrases taken from the research, each traceable to a source
- Reach: channels, content formats and topics, trusted communities and voices

Every persona states its evidence base (number and kind of sources) and the date. Review it every quarter, because markets and products move.
